Method

How the backtest works

  1. 01

    Replay your tickets

    We take your closed tickets from the last 90 days and check out your repo at the commit just before each fix landed.

  2. 02

    Solve each one blind

    Our software factory, an orchestration of agents we built, solves every ticket inside a sealed container: no network beyond the model API, no access to the real fix, nothing but that checkout.

  3. 03

    Compare, honestly

    Your test suite runs on both patches. A blind judge compares the software factory's patch to your engineer's.

Next

What comes after the backtest

  • A classifier built from your results.We train a classifier on the backtest verdicts to decide which of your tickets the software factory is equipped to take, and which stay with your team.
  • A three-month pilot on live tickets.The factory picks up live tickets from your queue as they arrive. You are charged per ticket successfully merged, and nothing for the rest.
  • An agent-friendly repo.After the backtest we make your repo agentic friendly: reproducible environments, clear test entry points, and the context agents need to work in your codebase without hand-holding.
