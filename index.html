Method

How the backtest works

  1. 01

    Replay your tickets

    We take your closed tickets from the last 90 days and check out your repo at the commit just before each fix landed.

  2. 02

    Solve each one blind

    Our software factory, an orchestration of agents we built, solves every ticket inside a sealed container: no network beyond the model API, no access to the real fix, nothing but that checkout.

  3. 03

    Compare, honestly

    Your test suite runs on both patches. A blind judge compares the software factory's patch to your engineer's.

Walkthrough

One ticket, start to finish

Scroll to follow three tickets out of Jira, into sealed containers, and through to a verdict. The tickets, code and results below are illustrative.

01 · Find

Read 90 days of closed tickets

We start from the tickets your team closed in the last 90 days, straight from Jira.

02 · Pick

Rank the ones we can score

A ticket is scorable when a fix was merged and tests touch the changed code. Here we follow three.

03 · Seal

One sealed container per ticket

Each ticket gets its own sandbox: your repo, no network beyond the model API, and no view of the real fix.

04 · Solve

Solve it on the repo as it was

The repo is checked out at the commit before the fix landed. The agent works from the ticket alone.

05 · Compare

Two patches for the same ticket

Our patch on the left. The patch your engineer merged on the right.

06 · Test and judge

Run the affected tests, then a blind judge

The harness runs the tests that touch the change on both patches. A judge that sees only A and B picks the better one.

07 · Report

Every ticket, scored honestly

Wins, ties and losses side by side, failures first.
