How the backtest works

01

Replay your tickets

We take your closed tickets from the last 90 days and check out your repo at the commit just before each fix landed.

02

Solve each one blind

An agent solves every ticket inside a sealed container: no network beyond the model API, no access to the real fix, nothing but that checkout.

03

Compare, honestly

Your test suite runs on both patches. A blind judge compares the agent's patch to your engineer's. Failures go on page one, never in a footnote.

Why the measurement is the product

  • Nobody buys outcome pricing on a demo. You see the agent on your code, your tickets, your tests, before a single invoice.
  • Every ticket is accounted for. Tickets that produce no patch, break the build, or time out are counted in the denominator, not dropped.
  • Every run is reproducible. Pinned model, pinned environment, frozen prompts, full transcripts. Rerun it and get the same answer.
  • Contamination is audited. Every solve is scanned for any path to the real fix. A contaminated ticket is shown and never scored.
