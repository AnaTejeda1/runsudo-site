Patch A wins on correctness, risk and scope.

Scope and craft: Patch B includes unrelated formatting churn in `warpc.go` (moved closing parens and argument wrapping) and in the dart-sass append. This obscures the fix. Patch A is focused and consistent with the surrounding code.
